4.1.11 (26/10/2026) - BO CAI DAY DU
- KICH BAN GHIM GIO HANG (muc moi trong app): mo clip xem nhu YouTube (Phat/Dung, phim cach, tua, lui/toi 1-5 giay), dung dung cho -> bam "Cam co tai day" -> chon TEN san pham (tu gio Chrome hoac dan ten) + giu bao lau (mac dinh 60 giay, it nhat 3 giay) -> Luu. Kich ban luu rieng tung clip (doi cho file van nhan), co Xuat/Nhap de chep may khac, Dan kich ban tu Notepad/Excel.
  Xem truoc mac dinh TAT TIENG va khong dung toi NBL VIDEO.
- GHIM CHEN khi LIVE: toi giay da cam co thi ghim xao tron tam dung, NBL ghim mon chen (tim theo TEN), giu dung thoi gian, roi ghim xao tron ghim ngay mon ke tiep. Ghim loi thi thu lai moi 5 giay trong thoi gian cua co. Clip lap thi kich ban lap. LIVE bat tay van chay neu NBL VIDEO dang phat.
- Gio xong NBL so ten mon trong kich ban voi gio that, mon nao khong co thi bao truoc gio LIVE.
- Ghim xao tron: de 0 ca 4 o (hoac khong chon mon) = TAT.
- Sua loi khoanh "So thu tu" khong nhan: so thu tu cua TikTok la O NHAP SO, NBL gio doc gia tri cua o do. Mon dang ghim nhay len khung "Da ghim" van thao duoc.
- Bo muc khoanh "Ten bo san pham" (khong dung toi; them gio tu doc danh sach bo).
- Nut "Phat vao cap ao" (Video & am thanh): NBL VIDEO phat vao VB-CABLE -> loa may im, LIVE van co tieng (Studio lay am thanh tu CABLE Output).

            5:00 | 3 | 2:00    (phut 5 ghim so 3 trong 2 phut)
